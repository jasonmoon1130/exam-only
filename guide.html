How to sign up for an AP exam as a self-studier

You can take an AP exam without taking the class. If your school doesn't offer it, you register through a school that does.

  1. Pick your exams and check the dates

    Look up the official exam dates on AP Central. If two of your exams are at the same time, you can still take both. The AP coordinator arranges late testing for one of them.

  2. Ask your own school first

    Even if your school doesn't teach the class, its AP coordinator may add you to the school's order as an exam-only student. Ask your counselor who the coordinator is. The College Board explains this on its page about exams your school doesn't offer.

  3. Find schools that might host you

    Use Find schools to list the high schools near you, and the AP Course Ledger to check which ones are authorized to teach your course. Build a list of 10 to 25 schools within driving distance.

    Some schools won't take outside students, so plan to work down the list.

  4. Email several schools at once

    Write to the AP coordinator, the testing coordinator or an AP counselor. Send to several schools the same day instead of waiting on one reply. Expect rejections before you get a yes. It can take dozens of emails.

  5. Move fast after a yes

    The same day a school agrees:

    • Confirm which exams you're taking, and mention any date conflicts.
    • Ask the total cost and the payment deadline. You usually pay the host school, not the College Board.
    • Get their registration steps and follow them exactly.

If schools keep saying no

  • Widen your search. On Find schools, search by ZIP code to see every school within 40 miles.
  • Try private and religious schools. They're often more flexible.
  • Ask your school district's office. They usually know which school in the area hosts AP testing.
  • Call AP Services for Students at 888-225-5427 (weekdays, 8 am to 8 pm Eastern; outside the U.S., +1-212-632-1780) or use their contact form. They may be able to give you the numbers of nearby coordinators who test outside students.

Late testing

If you can't make the regular exam date, ask your AP coordinator about late testing. If late testing doesn't work either, there's a second option called exception testing. Your coordinator handles both.

If you can't register at all

Take the class free online through AP Access for ALL, try dual enrollment at a local college, or take your school's hardest courses. Colleges don't penalize you for what your school doesn't offer.

Two last tips

  • Start early. Schools order exams by November 13, 2026, and many want your payment before that.
  • Budget for fees. The exam is $99 in the U.S. for 2026–27, and some schools add their own fee.
