Key dates

From the College Board's 2026–27 calendar. Your host school may want things sooner.

    Schools order exams

    Get a school to say yes before then.

  2. March 12, 2027

    Last day for late orders

    Schools can still add you, but late orders cost extra.

  3. May 3 to 7, 2027

    AP exams, week 1

    Look up your exam's day on the official schedule.

  4. May 10 to 14, 2027

    AP exams, week 2

    Two exams at the same time? Ask your coordinator about late testing.

Source: AP Central exam dates and 2026–27 key dates.

AP in Tennessee

Before 2021, half of Tennessee high school students had no AP course at their school. The state now teaches 22 AP courses free online through AP Access for ALL, but as of May 2026 only 48% of eligible rural schools were taking part.

Sources: Tennessee Department of Education, AP Access for ALL, State Board of Education.
