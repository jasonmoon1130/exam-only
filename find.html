    Before you email

    1. Ask your own school first. Your AP coordinator can add you to the school's exam order without the class.
    2. Email five schools the same day. Don't wait for one reply before you try the next.
    3. Answer a yes the same day. Confirm the exam, the fee and their payment deadline.
